## Governed invariants

- Every Publication belongs to one active Workspace.
- Every Publication has one primary Story in the same Workspace.
- Cross-Workspace and archived-Story provenance is rejected.
- Lifecycle transitions are `Draft → InReview → Approved`, with review return to
  Draft and approved return to Draft before editing.
- Approved and archived Publications are read-only.
- Every accepted mutation increments the optimistic version, creates an immutable
  checkpoint, and appends a durable event in one transaction.
- Archive removes the Publication from active search; restore returns it to Draft
  and rebuilds search through the accepted trigger.
- Archived Workspaces block Publication mutation.
